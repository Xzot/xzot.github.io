Mirror Wings

Half a butterfly is done. Your child finishes the other half, and it flies off into the garden.

  • Ages 4–8
  • iPhone and iPad
  • No ads
  • No in-app purchases
  • Works offline

Each picture shows one side of a butterfly, a beetle, an owl, a little house or a crystal. Your child drags spots and paint onto the empty side so both halves match, and draws the missing lines. When the halves match, the creature comes alive: the butterfly opens its wings, the owl winks, the house lights its windows. Then it moves into the garden, where every creature your child finished keeps its own pattern.

There is nothing to read and no way to lose. You pay once; everything is included.

What's inside

Thirteen shapesButterflies, a moth, a beetle, a ladybug, an owl, a cat, a house, a boat and a tree reflected in a lake, a flower and a crystal. The spots are different every time.
Three kinds of mirrorLeft and right first, then top and bottom (a reflection in water), then both at once: one quarter is given, three are left to make.
It adapts on its ownAfter three pictures without a slip, the next ones get richer: more spots, two sizes, similar colours, a line to draw. After a tricky one, it eases off again.
Help without wordsA piece that lands in the wrong place glides back to the tray. After two tries the mirror line glows and the original spot floats across to show where its twin goes. There are no buzzers, no timer and no score.
A garden that growsFive scenes open one after another: a meadow, a pond, a starry night, winter and a little town. Tap a creature and it dances.
The magic mirrorFree drawing where whatever your child draws appears in the mirror at the same time: left and right, top and bottom, four corners, or a kaleidoscope with 6, 8 or 12 sides. Pictures go into an album inside the app.

Questions

How do I open the grown-ups section?

Tap the small gear in the top corner of the garden and answer the multiplication question, for example 14 × 6. A new question appears after a wrong answer.

It's too easy or too hard.

The game adjusts by itself, but you can set the level (1 to 8) in the grown-ups section. Level 1 is two big round spots; level 8 mixes everything with tighter placement.

How do I save a drawing to Photos?

Open the album in the magic mirror, tap a drawing, then the save button. Saving to Photos is for grown-ups, so the parental gate appears first. The app asks only to add pictures and never reads your library.

There is no sound.

Sounds follow the silent switch. Check that it is off, that the volume is up, and that Sounds is on in the grown-ups section.

How do I watch the intro again or start over?

Both are in the grown-ups section: "Show the intro again" and "Start over". Starting over empties the garden; drawings in the album stay.

Can the whole family use it?

Yes. Family Sharing is on, so everyone in your family group can download it at no extra cost.
