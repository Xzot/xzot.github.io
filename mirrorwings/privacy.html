Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

In short: Mirror Wings collects nothing. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no third-party code, and the app makes no network requests.

What stays on your device

Game progress (how many pictures were finished, the difficulty level and the creatures in the garden), drawings saved in the in-app album, and settings (sounds, volume, vibration) are stored only on your iPhone or iPad. We never receive them. Deleting the app deletes them.

Photos

A grown-up can save a drawing to the Photos library after passing the parental gate. For that the app asks for permission to add photos only. It cannot see, read or upload anything in your library, and it never uses the camera or the microphone.

Children's privacy

Mirror Wings is made for children and is offered in the Kids Category of the App Store. We collect no personal information from anyone, including children, so there is nothing to share or sell, and nothing to target ads with.

  • The app never asks for a name, photo, voice recording or location.
  • No third party receives any information from the app. It contains no ads, analytics or other third-party code.
  • Settings, links to outside websites and saving to Photos are behind a parental gate, so a child cannot reach them by accident.
  • To delete your child's progress, use Start over in the grown-ups section, or delete the app.

Buying the app

Mirror Wings is bought through the App Store. Apple handles payment; we never see your payment details or Apple Account. There are no purchases inside the app.

If you write to us

If you email us, we see your email address and your message and use them only to answer you.
