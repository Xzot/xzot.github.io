Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Big Whipper doesn't collect any data about you. Nothing you do in the game is sent to me or to anyone else.

  1. 1

    What stays on your phone

    The game keeps your stars and finished routes, the look you picked, feet or meters, sound, haptics and the Assist setting. All of it lives only on your iPhone. I never receive it. Delete the app and it's gone.

  2. 2

    What leaves your phone

    Nothing, unless you send it yourself. The game has no account and no server. It contains no analytics, no ads, no crash reporter and no code from other companies, and it doesn't track you across apps or websites. The Daily Route is worked out on the phone from the date, so there's nothing to download.

  3. 3

    Fall clips and Photos

    A clip of a fall is made on your phone. It goes to Photos or to another app only when you tap save or share. When you first save one, iOS asks if the game may add to your photo library. That permission is add-only: the game can't see, read or upload anything already in your library.

  4. 4

    Camera, microphone, location

    The game never uses any of them, and it never asks.

  5. 5

    Buying the game

    You buy Big Whipper through the App Store. Apple handles the payment and your Apple Account, and I never see your card or payment details. Apple's own privacy policy covers that part.

  6. 6

    Children

    The game collects no personal information from anyone, children included, so there's nothing to share or sell.

  7. 7

    If you email me

    I'll see your email address and whatever you write. I use them only to answer you.

  8. Anchor

    Changes and contact

    If this policy changes, the new version goes on this page with a new date. Questions go to shchukavlad4@gmail.com. Developer: Vladyslav Shchuka.
