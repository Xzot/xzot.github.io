Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

In short: Big Whipper collects nothing. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no third-party code. Your progress stays on your iPhone.

What stays on your device

Your stars, completed routes, settings (look, assist mode, sound) and which purchases the app has seen are stored only on your iPhone. We never receive them. Deleting the app deletes them.

No servers, no tracking

The game has no backend of its own. We don't run analytics, we don't use advertising or crash-reporting libraries, and we don't track you across apps or websites. The Daily Route is worked out on the phone from the date, so it needs no download.

Clips and Photos

The game can record a fall and turn it into a short video. The video is made on your phone. It goes to your Photos library or to another app only if you tap save or share. When you save, iOS asks for permission to add to your photo library. That permission is add-only: the app can't see, read or upload anything already in your library. The app never uses the camera, the microphone or your location.

Purchases

Crag packs and the All Access subscription are bought through the App Store. Apple handles payment and your Apple Account; we never see your card or payment details. The app asks Apple only whether your account owns a pack or has an active subscription, so it can unlock the right content.

Children

We collect no personal information from anyone, including children, so there is nothing to share or sell. The app contains no ads and no links that collect data.

If you write to us

If you email us, we see your email address and your message and use them only to answer you.

Changes and contact
