You move every limb yourself. There's no climb button. It's your thumbs on the wall, and the pump in your forearms keeps building until you rest or let go.

  1. P1

    Start

    Big Whipper is a physics climbing game for iPhone, played in portrait. It runs on iOS 18 or later.

    This is the support page. It covers how the game works, what's in it, and what to do when something goes wrong. If your question isn't here, email me at the bottom of the page.

  2. P2

    The moves

    Touch a hand or a foot and drag it toward a hold. When you lift your finger, it grabs the hold inside the green ring. Drag the body itself to move your weight.

    The climber is a simulated body, not an animation. Grip fades the longer you hang on a hold. Your forearms pump on steep ground, and stamina drains over a long route. Feet count for a lot: weight a good foothold and your arms get a break. Leave a foot swinging and you'll pay for it two moves later.

    Too hard at first? Switch on Assist in Settings. The grab range gets bigger, the arms get stronger, and the pump comes on slower.

  3. P3

    Crags

    Three crags, 12 routes each, graded on the French scale from 4a to 9a. Every route has three stars to earn.

    • Morning Slab 12 routes Where you learn to trust your feet. Big holds, easier angles.
    • Red Quarry, The Spire 12 routes each Longer routes and harder moves than the slab.
    • Boulders 20 problems · V0–V12 Short and powerful, no rope. You land on a crash pad.
    • Daily Route 1 a day A new wall each day, the same one for everybody. Past days stay in the archive, so you can go back for the ones you missed.
    • Weekly bonus 1 a week An extra route that changes every week.

    Two other ways to play the same walls. Free Solo takes the rope away. Two Hands puts two people on one phone: one plays the left side of the body, the other plays the right. When the climber comes off, the game tells you whose side let go.

  4. P4

    Falling

    You will fall a lot, and that's part of the game. When the grip goes, the climber drops until the rope comes tight on the last quickdraw you clipped. Clip often and you barely move. Run it out past a bolt and you get the big one, the whipper the game is named after.

    Your belayer usually has something to say about it. Then you see the fall again in slow motion.

    Any fall can be turned into a short vertical video (9:16). It's made on your phone and goes nowhere until you save it or share it. The first time you save one, iOS asks if the game may add to your Photos. That permission is add-only, so the game can't see what's already in your library.

  5. P5

    Looks and settings

    On first launch you pick how the game is painted: Golden Hour, Bold Comic or Alpine. The walls and the physics don't change, only the paint. The app icon switches to match. You can change your pick any time in Settings.

    Settings is also where you choose feet or meters and turn sound, haptics and Assist on or off.

  6. P6

    Help

    I keep getting stuck on one move

    Look down before you look up. A higher foot makes most long reaches possible. Try the move a few different ways, since falling costs you nothing. If it still won't go, turn on Assist in Settings.

    Where is my progress saved?

    Only on this iPhone. There's no account and no sync. If you delete the app, your stars and settings go with it.

    A clip won't save to Photos

    You probably said no to the Photos prompt the first time. Open the iPhone Settings app, find Big Whipper (under Apps), tap Photos and choose Add Photos Only. Then save the clip again.

    I want a refund

    Apple handles every App Store payment, so I can't refund anything myself. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple Account and pick Big Whipper.

    Something is broken

    Email me. Tell me your iPhone model, your iOS version, which route you were on and what happened. A screen recording helps a lot.
