Big Whipper

Each thumb moves one hand. Reach, grab, pull. When the grip goes, the rope catches you.

  • iPhone
  • No ads
  • No account
  • No tracking
  • Works offline

Put a thumb down and a hand follows it. Lift it and the hand grabs the nearest hold. The climber's body is fully simulated, so a lazy foot or a pumped forearm changes everything. Sooner or later you fall, the rope catches you at the last bolt you clipped, and the replay shows the whole flight in slow motion. Any fall can become a short vertical clip to send to a friend.

What's inside

Real physicsNo auto-climb. Balance, grip strength and the position of your feet decide whether a move works.
Routes and bouldersMorning Slab has 12 routes with big holds. Red Quarry and The Spire are longer and harder. There are also 20 boulder problems over a crash pad, each with three stars to earn.
Daily RouteOne new wall every day, the same for everyone.
Two HandsTwo players on one phone, one hand each. The game tells you whose hand let go.
Three looksGolden Hour, Bold Comic and Alpine. Same wall and same physics, a different painting. Switch any time in Settings.
Falls you can shareThe game records every fall and turns it into a short vertical clip. It is saved or shared only when you choose to.

Questions

How do I control the climber?

Touch the screen with a thumb to guide a hand toward a hold, and lift to grab it. Use the other thumb for the other hand. Settings has an Assist mode with a bigger grab range and stronger arms.

What do I get with each purchase?

Morning Slab, today's Daily Route, all boulder problems, Two Hands and all three looks are open to everyone, and the first route of every other crag is a sample. Red Quarry and The Spire are sold as one-time crag packs. All Access is an auto-renewing subscription, monthly or yearly, that opens every crag (new ones too), Free Solo, the archive of past Daily Routes and a bonus route each week. Prices are shown in the app before you buy.

How do I cancel All Access?

On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose Big Whipper and tap Cancel. Cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends or it renews. You keep access until the period runs out. Crag packs you bought stay yours either way.

I bought something and it's missing.

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought with. Family Sharing applies to the crag packs.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles all payments and refunds. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, and request a refund for the purchase. We can't issue refunds ourselves.

Where is my progress stored?

Only on your iPhone. There is no account and no server, so deleting the app deletes your stars and settings. Purchases are tied to your Apple Account, not the device.

How do I save a clip of a fall?

After a fall, use the save or share button on the replay screen. You can save the clip to Photos or send it from the share sheet. The first time you save, iOS asks permission to add to your photo library. The app can only add clips; it can't see or read anything else in your library.

The climber feels too hard to control.

Try Assist mode in Settings. If a route still feels stuck, a fall costs nothing and the rope always catches you. Free Solo, for subscribers, removes the rope.

Something isn't working.

Write to shchukavlad4@gmail.com with your iPhone model, the iOS version and what happened. A screenshot or screen recording helps.
